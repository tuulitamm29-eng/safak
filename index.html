Flower Guide

What flowers are, what they do, and which ones grow near you.

This is a curated guide of about 50 well-known flowers, not every flower on Earth (there are roughly 300,000 flowering plant species). "Near me" shows flowers that grow wild or are commonly seen in the country you pick. Traditional uses are not medical advice. Never eat a wild plant unless an expert has identified it, and keep poisonous ones away from children and pets.
